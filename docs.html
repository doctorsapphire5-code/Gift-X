Frequently asked questions

Is VYRO free?

Yes. The Free plan has no time limit. See Pricing for paid plans.

How do I reset my password?

On the Log in page, enter your email and select Forgot your password. We will email you a reset link.

Can I sign in with Google?

Yes. Select Continue with Google on the Log in page.

How do I contact support?

Use the Contact page and we will reply by email.
